Welcome to my little corner of the internet.

I'm a girl who loves stories, pretty clothes, good films, books, music and all the tiny details that make ordinary days feel a little more magical.

I created The Witty Muse as a place to collect the things I love — from fashion and films to books, songs, thoughts and little pieces of everyday life.

Think of it as a personal diary, a tiny magazine, and a collection of things I would probably send to my best friend at midnight.
